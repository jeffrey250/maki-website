- Our services -

Environmental Monitoring & Water Sampling

Data you can trust for better decisions

We collect real-time spatial water quality data and targeted samples using autonomous systems fitted with sensors and sampling equipment for lakes, rivers, ponds and coastal environments.

Bathymetry

Accurate underwater depth mapping

USV-based sonar surveying creates reliable depth models for lakes, ponds, reservoirs, treatment plants, harbours and waterways while reducing safety risks and field effort.

Topography

High-resolution land and shoreline mapping

Drone and survey workflows capture landform, shoreline, vegetation and terrain information for planning, monitoring and environmental management.

Bathymetry & Topography

Integrated land and water surveys

Combined topo-bathymetric mapping gives clients a single joined picture across water and land, simplifying coastal, lake, wetland and infrastructure projects.

Stormwater & Treatment Pond Surveys

Built for councils and infrastructure managers

We survey stormwater retention ponds, treatment ponds and detention basins using autonomous surface vessels — giving councils accurate sediment volume data, bathymetric maps and condition reports without diver risk or disruption to operations.

Water Reservoir Inspection

Safe inspection without dewatering or confined space entry

MĀKI deploys underwater ROVs and surface rovers to inspect water reservoir infrastructure — capturing high-resolution video and imagery of submerged walls, floors and inlet/outlet structures without the need to dewater or send staff into confined spaces.

Asset Management (Inspection)

Smarter infrastructure monitoring

ROVs, USVs, drones and imaging systems inspect hard-to-reach assets including submerged structures, bridges, dams, pipes and monitoring equipment.
