- Our Vehicles -

Versatile in application. Fluid in approach. Unwavering in spirit.

MĀKI designs, develops, builds and operates unmanned systems across air, surface and underwater environments. Each platform is purpose-built for the mission — from aerial photogrammetry to deep-water inspection and autonomous water sampling.

- Our unmanned platforms -

MĀKI SkyDrop

Waterproof Sampling Drone

The SkyDrop enables rapid water sampling from isolated or hazardous areas like ponds, flood zones, and sediment basins. Built on a SwellPro waterproof platform, it delivers samples to locations that are difficult or unsafe to reach by boat or on foot.

MĀKI AeroMap

Aerial 3D Mapping & Photogrammetry

Powered by the DJI Matrice 4E, the AeroMap captures high-resolution aerial imagery for land-use planning, construction monitoring and environmental assessment. Data integrates with water-based surveys for unified 3D models.

MĀKI Hydromap

Autonomous Bathymetry USV — Boat V1

The Hydromap collects high-resolution sonar data for depth profiling, sedimentation assessment and pond management. Compact and fully autonomous, it operates in lakes, ponds and reservoirs with minimal setup.

MĀKI Hydrohub

Smart USV for Water Sampling, Monitoring & Bathymetry — Boat V2

The Hydrohub performs real-time water quality monitoring, multi-point sampling and bathymetric mapping using sonar and GPS. Winch-deployed sensors allow profiling at multiple depths in a single mission.

MĀKI DeepView

Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV)

The DeepView is an underwater inspection tool used for dam walls, nets, pipes and submerged structures. It provides live video and sonar to operators on the surface, reducing the need for divers in hazardous environments.

MĀKI MicroScan

Compact Sonar Boat for Tight & Shallow Areas

The MicroScan is a lightweight, remote-controlled surface vessel fitted with a sonar system, ideal for drainage ponds, culverts and sediment basins where larger vessels cannot operate.

MĀKI SludgeScan

Dual-Frequency Multi-Beam Sludge & Sediment Survey USV

The SludgeScan is a purpose-built autonomous surface vessel equipped with dual-frequency and multi-beam sonar, designed specifically for high-accuracy sludge and sediment surveys. It delivers precise volume and depth data in treatment ponds, settling ponds, lakes and reservoirs — enabling operators to make confident dredging and maintenance decisions.

MĀKI Rover

Unmanned Ground Vehicle (UGV)

The MĀKI Rover is a compact unmanned ground vehicle designed to access small, confined and hard-to-reach spaces. Equipped with a sensor mast, it delivers visual and environmental data from locations inaccessible to larger platforms or personnel.
