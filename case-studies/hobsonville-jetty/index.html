Bathymetry & Topography

Underwater 3D Mapping & Jetty Survey at Hobsonville Point

← Case Studies / Hobsonville Point Jetty Survey
ClientAuckland Council Healthy Waters & Flood Resilience
PartnerGlasgow Contractors
LocationHobsonville Point, Auckland
PlatformMĀKI USV (multibeam sonar) + RTK drone

Overview

At Hobsonville Point in Auckland, MĀKI partnered with Glasgow Contractors to complete a detailed bathymetric and topographic survey within a tight timeframe. The work was carried out on behalf of Auckland Council's Healthy Waters and Flood Resilience department.

Using MĀKI's in-house designed and built uncrewed surface vessel (USV) fitted with multibeam sonar, alongside RTK drone photogrammetry, the team captured detailed above-water and underwater survey data and combined it into an integrated 3D model of the site.

The Challenge

The survey area extended underneath a jetty — a confined space that conventional crewed survey boats struggle to access. The project also had a tight timeframe, so the team needed to mobilise quickly and capture complete data in one campaign.

Our Approach

Multibeam sonar bathymetry. MĀKI's purpose-built USV mapped the underwater terrain in detail with multibeam sonar.

Under-jetty surveying. MĀKI's USV pilot navigated the vessel beneath the jetty, collecting survey data in confined spaces that would be challenging for conventional crewed vessels.

RTK drone photogrammetry. An RTK-positioned drone captured the exposed coastal structures and the surrounding topography.

Integrated 3D modelling. The above-water and underwater datasets were combined into one model, giving a more complete picture of the infrastructure and its surrounding environment.
