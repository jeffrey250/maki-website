Drinking-Water Reservoir Inspection, Westport

← Case Studies / Westport Reservoir Inspection
ClientBuller District Council
LocationWestport, West Coast
AssetDrinking-water reservoir
PlatformUnderwater ROV + Rover

Overview

For Buller District Council, MĀKI inspected a drinking-water reservoir in Westport using two robots: an underwater ROV that surveyed the inside of the reservoir, and a rover that carried out the inspection on top of it. The reservoir did not have to be drained, and nobody had to go inside.

The Challenge

Water reservoirs are critical infrastructure assets that require periodic condition assessment to identify structural deterioration, sediment accumulation, biofilm growth and damage to inlet and outlet mechanisms. Conventional inspection approaches typically require the reservoir to be taken offline and dewatered — a costly, time-consuming process that disrupts water supply operations and can take days or weeks to complete.

Where dewatering is avoided, the alternative is to have staff enter the confined, potentially oxygen-depleted space of a partially drained reservoir — a significant health and safety risk requiring extensive hazard management procedures. The council needed a safer approach that could inspect the reservoir with the water left in it.

Our Approach

Inside the reservoir — underwater ROV. MĀKI's ROV was piloted underwater through the reservoir, filming the interior with its lights and HD camera. Its live video feed let the team spot anything of interest in real time and steer in for a closer look.

On top of the reservoir — rover. The MĀKI Rover, a compact unmanned ground vehicle with a camera mast, carried out the inspection on top of the reservoir. Together the two robots gave the council a visual record of the asset, inside and out.
