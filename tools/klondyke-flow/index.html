River flow events

The Rangitata normally runs down the far side of the gravel at Klondyke Bend. Now and then it swings across to the Klondyke Terrace side. Those swings are what put the road at risk, and they happen long before the bank itself shows any change — so they are worth watching on their own.

Where the river has been running

Every satellite pass, scored from 0 to 1: 0 means the flow is hard against Klondyke Terrace, 1 means it is against the far bank. Measured as a share of the gravel’s width, so it stays comparable where the braidplain is wide at one end of the bend and narrow at the other. Shaded bands are the events picked out below.
