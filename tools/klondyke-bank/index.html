Bank movement measurement

Draw a line from each surveyed control point out to the bank, then mark where the bank sat in each year. Retreat and rate are worked out per line, and compared.
1.0× Scroll to zoom · drag to pan · double-click to zoom in
Working on CP 1 — click out at the bank to set the line.
Imagery: LINZ Canterbury aerial, CC BY 4.0. Control points: RDR 049 Bridge 3, NZTM2000. Scene is 775 × 707 m.

How to measure

1Pick a control point (green) — the fixed, surveyed end of the line.
2Click out at the bank to set that line's direction and length.
3Pick a year, then click on the line where the bank was that year.
Repeat for the other control points — three lines along the frontage show whether the bank is retreating evenly.

Lines

One line per control point. Click a control point on the map, or a button here, to switch. Each keeps its own marks.
