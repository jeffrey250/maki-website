Omokoroa stormwater ponds

Surveyed surfaces for eleven council assets. Each pond opens a 3D model with a cross-section tool. The badge is the share of the pond within 3 m of an actual measurement — these ponds were surveyed by different methods, from 42 RTK staff shots to 293,000 soundings, and the viewer shows that rather than hiding it.

Survey estimates for planning and prioritising — not a quantity take-off for pricing or tender. Pricing sediment removal needs a targeted probe survey of the pond concerned.

Scope and limitations
  • Every figure here is taken from the survey report already issued for that pond.
  • Before removal is priced, a probe or core grid across the area to be removed fixes the base of the removable material — a harder-driven probe or a core can find further material beneath the surface these surveys record.
  • The ponds were not surveyed to the same density, from 42 staff readings to 293,000 soundings. The badge on each pond gives its own coverage; treat a sparse pond as indicative.
  • Water standing on survey day is not the water quality volume, and the two should not be compared directly.
  • A survey is a snapshot — these ponds continue to silt. Each page states its date.

Happy to scope a detailed survey on any pond here — usually a morning on site per pond.
