Lakes Mapping for Waikato Regional Council

Dec 31, 20221 min read

MĀKI is now contracted to do lakes bathymetric mapping for the Waikato Regional Council in New Zealand. This involves using specialised equipment to measure and map the depths of lakes and other bodies of water.

MĀKI has developed autonomous boats that we use for these projects, which allows us to efficiently and accurately gather data from the lakes.

MĀKI is grateful to be working with the Waikato Regional Council on this important work and hopes there will be more opportunities for collaboration in the future to improve the water quality of the region's lakes. We are excited and committed to using our skills and expertise to help protect and enhance the natural beauty of New Zealand.
